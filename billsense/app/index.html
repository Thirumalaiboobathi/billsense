How Billsense protects your data

  • Read-only IAM role. Allowed actions: ce:GetCostAndUsage ec2:Describe* bedrock:InvokeModel bedrock:InvokeModelWithResponseStream (Nova Pro only). Nothing can be changed or deleted.
  • No AWS keys accepted. Keys pasted here are blocked before they reach the model or any log.
  • Account and resource IDs masked. Only the last 4 characters are ever shown.
  • Nothing stored server-side. No database; questions and answers are not logged.
  • Rate-limited. Too many requests or wrong passcodes are temporarily blocked.
Billsense never asks for AWS access keys. It reads cost data through a read-only IAM role.
